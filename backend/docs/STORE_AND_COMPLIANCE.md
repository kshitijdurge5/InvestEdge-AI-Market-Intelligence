# Store and compliance checklist

This is an engineering checklist, not legal advice.

## Google Play

For a financial/investment app, use the Organization developer account path and complete the required verification.

Prepare:
- organization identity
- D-U-N-S where required
- verified contact details
- website
- privacy policy URL
- terms URL
- financial features declaration
- accurate app category and disclosures
- data safety declarations
- permissions justification
- account deletion flow
- customer support/contact method

## Apple

Prepare:
- Apple Developer Program membership
- App Store Connect organization/account information
- privacy policy
- App Privacy disclosures
- account deletion capability where required
- support URL
- age rating
- financial-service disclosures
- TestFlight testing
- production review build

## India / financial regulation

Before public release, get Indian regulatory counsel to determine whether the product is:
- market information/education,
- a Research Analyst service,
- an Investment Adviser service,
- or another regulated activity.

Do not market AI-generated BUY/HOLD/SELL or personalized portfolio recommendations as regulated investment advice until the applicable registration/partner structure is confirmed.

## Market data

Confirm the commercial licensing terms for every live market-data source used in the consumer app.

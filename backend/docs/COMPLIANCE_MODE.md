INVESTEDGE COMPLIANCE-FIRST PRODUCT MODE
========================================

Purpose
-------
Build and test the product now while keeping a clear switch between:
1) RESEARCH_ONLY — market data, charts, technical/fundamental analysis and educational explanations.
2) ADVISORY — personalized investment advice / recommendations, enabled only after the business has completed the legal and regulatory review required for the actual service.

IMPORTANT
---------
This is an engineering control, not a legal opinion. Before public launch of any feature that recommends, advises, or solicits transactions in securities, obtain India-specific legal/compliance advice and confirm the applicable SEBI registration/requirements for the exact business model.

Recommended environment variable:
APP_COMPLIANCE_MODE=RESEARCH_ONLY

Never treat a disclaimer alone as a substitute for regulatory compliance.
